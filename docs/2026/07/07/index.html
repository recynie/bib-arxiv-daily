PTB-Search samples independent LLM proposals once, extracts reusable terms into a per-problem dictionary, and performs train-only set-level sparse selection with least-squares coefficients. Across these results, LLMs are best understood as material suppliers; discovery is carried by external set-level selection over reusable components. more
